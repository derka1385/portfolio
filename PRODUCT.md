# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People who admire innovative, modern websites: visitors who come to be transported for the length of a visit, and who judge the author by the experience itself (designers, builders, founders, recruiters and event contacts who arrive from a link, a CV or LinkedIn). They mostly arrive on a phone or a laptop, often from a shared link, with a few minutes of attention.

## Product Purpose

Nolann Petri's personal portfolio. It must make a visitor feel something and remember him: the site itself is the proof that he designs and builds experiences. Success is a visitor who stays until the end, understands who he is (founder of ORVECT, SSE student, builder, brand designer, car person) and wants to contact him.

## Positioning

A film in four reels (Code, Photo, Film, Design). The home is a pure scroll experience with no text past his name, built to show what he can code in design ("Lignes", chosen by Nolann on 27/09/2026). Only he can tell it with these projects: ORVECT, the Col de l'Est car association, the web apps he shipped, his car photography and films, the brand work, the move to the Stockholm School of Economics. The previous night-drive roadbook lives on as a separate experience in `~/Coding/plein-phares`.

## Operating Context

Static multi-page site, no build step (`index.html`, `code.html`, `photo.html`, `film.html`, `design.html`, `contact.html`, `style.css`, `site.js` plus one script per page, `img/`, `vid/`), raw WebGL2 on the home page with a plain name as fallback, English by default with a French toggle (`data-fr`), `prefers-reduced-motion` respected. Previewed as a private claude.ai artifact (iframes of live sites are blocked there by CSP; the site detects framing and opens new tabs instead). Intended for GitHub Pages later.

## Product Principles

1. The visit is the portfolio: every interaction must be worth feeling, not just reading.
2. His real route and real projects carry the story; nothing is invented.
3. Fewer things, shown bigger: curation over completeness.
4. Emotion never costs clarity: a visitor can always tell where they are and how to reach him.
