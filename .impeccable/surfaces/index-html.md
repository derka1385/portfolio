## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is a film by Nolann Petri: every page is a reel, every fact is a credit, and the interface lives in the letterbox bars around his own footage. It refuses the dark portfolio of a big name over an abstract WebGL blob followed by a card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Projection black bars around a 2.39:1 frame of his footage; credit white; one subtitle yellow for subtitles, the running timecode and the single action per view; anamorphic blue exists only as light. Archivo: titles in expanded caps, roles in condensed small caps; a mono for timecode and frame numbers. Components: title cards, credit blocks (role / name), subtitles, timecode, edit timeline with coloured clip tracks, stills with frame numbers, storyboard panels, end-credit roll.

STORY: Opening titles over Vosges drone footage, then scenes told in subtitles (the border, the machines, the code, Stockholm), the manifesto as an intertitle, the programme of four reels (Code as an editing suite, Photo as stills, Film as a projection room, Design as storyboards), and end credits that are the contact.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Black; a horizontal anamorphic streak sweeps across and leaves NOLANN PETRI in widely tracked expanded caps, small inside a vast dark frame; the letterbox opens onto drone footage following a car on a Vosges road. Top bar: name, the reels, subtitle track EN/FR, Contact. Bottom bar: running timecode and the first subtitle. Scrolling plays the film.

FORM: Le Générique (film title sequence), position 1 of my ordered list, chosen by Nolann from the pick card; seed key 473fa0f3.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Raises kept from the declined hand: every glow has a source, the pointer or a light in the footage (neon circuit); every layout composes inside the 2.39 frame and its title-safe area (azulejo whole-tile grid); every work carries the same fixed credit fields (character catalog); the language switch is a subtitle track (multilingual silkscreen); one scroll-driven timecode clock runs the motion (algorave); timecode digits roll per character (split-flap).

Signature interaction: the pointer is a light source; a WebGL pass over his footage draws an anamorphic flare, halation and grain where it points, and scroll velocity whip-pans the frame left or right between scenes. Motion grammar: cuts, whip-pans and fades to black, never slide-up card entrances.

## Home experience (Lignes), decided 27/09/2026

Nolann's brief for index.html: only his name at the start, then pure scroll experience; no text, no footage, no links to his work, no summary; it should show what he can code in design; natural, logical, strong; scroll must never feel too long or blurry. Surface round seed 372af4eb (structures dealt 3, 2, 6); Nolann locked "Lignes" (index 6 of my list).

THESIS: One exact grid of hairlines and one front of light. The front sweeps left to right and builds whatever it crosses (his name rises into walls, then a city of lines), turns at the far end and sweeps back folding everything flat, ending on the empty grid. It refuses the particle-screensaver and the video reel.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed black; seen from straight above, the grid draws itself out from the centre and NOLANN PETRI is traced on it in hairlines, segment by segment, like a plotter; a small non-text scroll cue. The chrome fades in after the trace.

STORY: Top-down plan, the front passes and the letters rise into walls while the camera tilts down to street level, slaloms left and right through the gaps between the letters, runs down an avenue while towers grow ahead of it, climbs until the city is tiny on an endless grid, then follows the returning front home as it folds the city and the name flat.

SIGNATURE: the front of light (a scanning plane, subtitle yellow where it touches lines, one blue anamorphic point at its source) is the only cause of change; the pointer lifts the grid like a finger under paper and lights the lines it passes. Motion grammar: springs with a little overshoot, hidden-line rendering so volumes have weight, no blur, a tight critically damped scroll response.
