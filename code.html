github.com/OliverVillson/LobBot00:00:00:00

LobBot

Tech Europe hackathon · 4th place · team of two · 2026

Context
An agent that turns a big general language model into a small model that does one job and runs offline on a laptop. Built as a team of two at the Tech Europe hackathon, where we finished 4th.
What I built
The whole front end and the brand: Dr. Lobbot, a 3D surgeon in Three.js with his own scenes; a SwiftUI iPhone app built around him that you can call through a Gemini Live voice; a stage version for the pitch; the presentation film. Oliver built the pipeline: pruning, healing, quantization, grading.
Goal
Make model compression feel like a visit to a funny doctor. On the demo job: 61 GB down to 6.52 GB, at 99% of the big model’s score.

SwiftUI · Three.js · Gemini Live · WebGL
