ORVECT

An AI copilot that helps independent garages go beyond the fault code.

Role
Founder · product, full stack, brand
Stack
FastAPI · Nebius · Tavily · Gemini · Firebase
Status
Prototype · Ignyte pre-seed
Since
2025 · Stockholm / Dubai

  1. One data point to start.

    A technician enters a VIN, a plate or the car by hand; ORVECT detects the configuration and has a human confirm it. Then come symptoms and fault codes, and ranked hypotheses, each with its source.

  2. Sources first, model second.

    A FastAPI pipeline runs web research with Tavily, then reasons on Nebius (gpt-oss-120b). Server-side checks drop invalid citations and reject degenerate rankings. I rebuilt it in one day at the Nebius × Tavily hackathon.

  3. Three strokes, one signal orange.

    A directional mark, Space Grotesk, graphite and mineral white. Rolled out as a brand guide, print and LinkedIn campaigns.

  4. A pitch you can scroll.

    A landing page with a scroll-driven vehicle scan, and a 16-slide investor deck published as a static site.
