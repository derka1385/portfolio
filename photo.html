Col de l’Est

Car association · founded 2025

An association that brings sports-car owners from France, Germany, Luxembourg and Switzerland onto the roads of Alsace and the Vosges passes. I planned the May 2025 rally end to end, in three languages, and I look after the pictures, the routes and the website.
